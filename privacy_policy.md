# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** June 10, 2026  
**App:** Home Maintenance Tracker  
**Developer:** Antonio Gutierrez Sanchez  
**Contact:** antogs99@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Home Maintenance Tracker handles information when you use the app.

## 1. Summary

Home Maintenance Tracker is designed to help you store and organize home maintenance information, appliance details, filter sizes, warranties, notes, photos, and reminders.

The app is intended to be local-first. In the current version, the developer does not collect, sell, rent, or share your personal data.

Your saved home information is stored on your device unless a future version clearly states otherwise or unless you use device-level services such as iCloud backup controlled by Apple.

## 2. Information You Add to the App

You may choose to enter information such as:

- Home names or room names
- Appliance details
- Model numbers and serial numbers
- HVAC filter sizes
- Warranty dates
- Maintenance notes
- Service history
- Contractor notes
- Photos
- Receipts or document notes
- Reminder dates
- Other home-related details

This information is created and controlled by you.

## 3. Data Collection by the Developer

The developer does not collect personal data from the app in the current version.

This means the developer does not receive or store your home records, photos, notes, reminders, appliance information, warranty details, or other content you enter into the app.

The developer also does not sell your data to advertisers or data brokers.

## 4. Local Device Storage

Your app data is stored locally on your device using the app’s device storage.

Because the data is stored locally, deleting the app may delete your app data. Losing, resetting, or damaging your device may also cause data loss if your device is not backed up.

You are responsible for maintaining your own backups through your device settings or any backup service you choose to use.

## 5. iCloud, Device Backup, and Apple Services

Depending on your device settings, Apple may back up app data through iCloud or device backups. These backups are controlled by Apple and your Apple ID settings, not by the developer.

Please review Apple’s privacy policy and iCloud settings to understand how Apple handles backup data.

## 6. Photos and Camera Access

The app may request access to your photo library or camera if you choose to add photos to home items.

Photo access is used only to let you select or save photos inside the app. Camera access is used only if you choose to take a photo from within the app.

The developer does not receive your photos.

You can change photo or camera permissions at any time in your device settings.

## 7. Notifications

The app may request permission to send notifications if you create maintenance reminders.

Notifications are used to remind you about tasks or dates you set in the app.

The developer does not receive your reminder content. You can disable notifications at any time in your device settings.

## 8. In-App Purchases

If you purchase a premium unlock, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store.

The developer does not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide developers with purchase-related information needed to validate purchases, manage entitlements, process refunds, or provide app functionality, subject to Apple’s own policies.

## 9. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of the app is intended not to use third-party analytics, advertising trackers, or cross-app tracking.

The app does not track you across other apps or websites for advertising purposes.

If analytics or crash reporting are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy and the App Store privacy details should be updated to describe what is collected and why.

## 10. Email Support

If you contact the developer by email, the developer may receive your email address and any information you choose to include in your message.

This information is used only to respond to your request, provide support, understand feedback, or resolve issues.

Do not send sensitive information by email unless you are comfortable sharing it.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

Home Maintenance Tracker is not directed to children under 13.

The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the developer, contact antogs99@gmail.com and the information will be deleted if required.

## 12. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

Data may be disclosed only if required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, or security.

## 13. Data Retention

Because app data is stored locally on your device, you control how long it is kept.

You can delete individual items in the app if the app provides that option. You can also delete the app from your device, which may delete locally stored app data.

Support emails may be retained as needed to respond to support requests, maintain records, or improve the app.

## 14. Your Choices

You can:

- Choose what information to enter into the app
- Delete app data from within the app where supported
- Delete the app from your device
- Disable notifications in device settings
- Change photo or camera permissions in device settings
- Contact the developer with privacy questions

## 15. Security

The app relies on Apple’s device security and app sandboxing to help protect locally stored data.

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. You are responsible for protecting access to your device, including using a passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, and device backups as appropriate.

## 16. International Users

The app is operated by Antonio Gutierrez Sanchez in the United States.

If you use the app outside the United States, you understand that support communications may be handled in the United States.

Your continued use of the app after the updated policy is posted means you accept the updated policy.
